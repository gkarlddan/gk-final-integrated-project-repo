Outdoor Information

Canada's New Official Bird; the Canada Jay and of course the Chick-a-dee! Touching Wildlife.

Famous birds that will eat seeds from your hand or your hat are the Canada Jay (aka the Gray Jay or the Whisky Jack) and Boreal Chickadee.

For the Aficionado Birder check the latest winter birding report for what is being seen and where.

  • Algonquin Logging Museum Trail

Tranquil with peacefulness. Algonquin Park has International quality ski trails; track set and skating lane.

48 kilometres of groomed and 23 kilometres of non-groomed cross-country ski trails available in three low-usage trail networks, typically December to early April. Check the latest ski trail conditions If you cannot go skiing you can always go IceHiking.

Provided Free to use:

  • Wax-less cross-country skis, boots and poles.
  • Waxing station for those that enjoy waxing.
  • Park Pass from our Office

IceHiking & Skating

IceHiking Information

Guaranteed Daily Winter Sport for you to enjoy nature! Touching Wildlife, Tranquil with peacefulness.

Fun and Easy and Safe. Invented Here in 2018.

IceHiking is a Guaranteed Winter Sport, We guarantee it daily - 7 days a week, All Winter long, November to April.

All Algonquin Park trails will now be easy to access even now in the Winter!

Authentic Canadian Experience of Skating Outdoors:

  • In Algonquin Park at Mew Lake Campground, rough surface like the "good ol days".
  • At the Township Rink, a short 15 minute walk into the Village, general skating or skirmish hockey (all invited).

Provided Free to Use:

  • Ice Skates up to size 14
  • Mew Lake Park Pass from our Office

Algonquin Visitor Center

Visitor Center Information

Pristine and natural snow covered Algonquin Park is home to world-class venues and We provide the Free Park Pass.

Algonquin Park's Visitor Centre is open daily, has exhibits on the Park's natural and human history, a relaxing restaurant, an excellent bookstore, theatre and a viewing deck of the Winter wild Algonquin landscape.
