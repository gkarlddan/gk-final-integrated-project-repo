SPA POLICIES AND INFORMATION

Policy Information

  • When you arrive, please wear loose, comfortable clothing if you are having facials. For body molding and body wraps, you will just have your robe and undergarments, and can change in the Treatment Room.
  • Appointments are available between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm. Appointments can be made for up to 2 people at a time, depending on accommodation size and availability of Spa Practitioners.
  • 100% of the full treatment price will be charged for cancellations or no-shows made less than twelve (12) hours prior to the appointment time.
  • For the best Algonquin Spa experience, please pre-book your appointment. While we strive to honor all bookings, unforeseen circumstances may affect scheduling. We will inform you promptly and work with you to find an alternative.
  • * Treatments are automatically charged to your account, plus 13% HST. Our NO EXTRA TIPPING policy ensures that your focus is on relaxation, not gratuities.
  • We have 6 Spa Treatment Rooms.

    Unforseen Circumstances

    Rarely, a Spa Professional may be unavailable due to illness or emergency. Staff will make every effort to provide an alternative time or treatment. If we cannot accommodate your appointment, no Spa charge will apply. A complimentary 1-hour (Non-RMT) Spa Treatment voucher will be provided for your next Couples Resort getaway.

    Thank you for your understanding,

    John Sorensen, President
